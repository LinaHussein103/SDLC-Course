What is a wireframe?

A simplified, usually grayscale blueprint of a screen. It shows structure, layout, content hierarchy and navigation — deliberately leaving out colour, imagery and polish.

Why wireframe before development?

Cheap to change

Move a box, not a component tree.

Aligns the team

Devs, designers & clients see the same plan.

Exposes gaps

Missing requirements and states show up early.

Testable early

Users can react before code exists.
