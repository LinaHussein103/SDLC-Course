Impact

Why UI/UX matters

Task completion

Can people actually finish what they came to do?

User satisfaction

Will they choose to come back?

Accessibility

Can people with disabilities use it at all?

Error rates

Confusing UI creates wrong data and support tickets.

Development cost

A fix in a sketch takes minutes; after release it takes sprints.

Customer retention

Users leave products that waste their time.

Business success

Adoption, reputation and cost all depend on whether real users succeed.
