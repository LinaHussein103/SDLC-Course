Mobile-first

Why mobile-first?

Design for the smallest, most constrained screen first, then enhance for larger ones. Constraints force you to decide what truly matters.

Limited screen space

No room for “nice to have”.

Content prioritisation

The primary task goes on top.

Touch interactions

Fingers, not precise mouse pointers.

Thumb-friendly navigation

Key actions within easy reach.

Responsive layouts

One design adapts to every width.

Progressive enhancement

Add columns & shortcuts as space grows.

Case-study hypothesis: we assume most students will use the library system on their phones. This is not a verified statistic about PSUT students — a real team would confirm it with surveys or analytics.
